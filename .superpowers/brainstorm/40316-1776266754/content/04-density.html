The trade-off

A. Spacious

  • Breathes — feels premium and calm
  • Large type, generous padding
  • Best for exec-level overviews
  • Less information per screen — more scrolling

B. Compact

  • More data visible at once — less scrolling
  • Grouped lists, segmented controls, progress bars
  • Best for daily operators who live in the tool
  • Tighter but still Apple-clean, not cramped
