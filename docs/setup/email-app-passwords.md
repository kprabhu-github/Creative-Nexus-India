# Connecting your @creativenexus.in mailbox to the dashboard

The dashboard uses your Workspace **app password** (not your normal Google login) to send and read mail on your behalf via SMTP + IMAP. App passwords are 16-character keys generated by Google specifically for non-browser apps.

This is a one-time setup, ~5 minutes per mailbox.

## Prerequisites

1. You must be signed in to your `@creativenexus.in` Google account.
2. **2-Step Verification must be enabled.** App passwords are only available on accounts with 2FA on.

## Step 1 — Enable 2-Step Verification (if not already on)

1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Under "How you sign in to Google", click **2-Step Verification**.
3. Follow the prompts (phone number → SMS code → confirm).
4. Verify the section now shows "2-Step Verification: On".

## Step 2 — Generate the app password

1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
2. If asked to sign in again, do so.
3. In the "App name" field, type: **Creative Nexus Dashboard**
4. Click **Create**.
5. A 16-character password appears in a yellow box, e.g. `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`.
6. **Copy it immediately** — you cannot view it again. (If you lose it, just generate a new one.)

## Step 3 — Connect in the dashboard

1. Go to https://creativenexusglobal.com/settings/integrations
2. Find your mailbox card (e.g. "tisha@creativenexus.in") and click **Connect**.
3. (Optional) Adjust the display name — this is what recipients see in the From field.
4. Paste the 16-char password into the App password field. Spaces are fine; the dashboard ignores them.
5. Click **Connect & save**.
6. The dashboard tests SMTP + IMAP login. If both succeed, the card flips to **Connected** ✓.

## What if it fails?

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "SMTP login failed: Username and Password not accepted" | Wrong password or 2FA not on | Confirm 2FA is on, generate a new app password, retry |
| "IMAP login failed" | IMAP disabled on the Gmail account | Gmail Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Enable IMAP → Save |
| "EMAIL_ENCRYPTION_KEY env var is missing" | Server misconfigured | Tell Prabhu — server-side fix |

## Security notes

- Your app password is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before being saved to the database.
- The dashboard never displays it again after save.
- To rotate, click **Re-enter password** on your card.
- To revoke from Google's side: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords → trash icon next to "Creative Nexus Dashboard". The dashboard's "Test" button will then mark the card as Failed.
